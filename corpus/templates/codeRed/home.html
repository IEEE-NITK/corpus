WHAT IS CODE RED?

Code Red is IEEE NITK Computer Society's annual flagship Capture The Flag (CTF) event, where competitors battle it out across multiple arenas including Algorithms, Systems, Machine Learning, Development, and much more.

This season, the pitch is bigger, the stakes are higher, and every challenge brings you one step closer to lifting the trophy. Welcome to Code Red - Season 3, where every flag signs a new player for your squad, every solve is a winning play, and only the best teams will make it to the top of the table.

The whistle has blown. Kick-off is here.

Are you ready to bring the trophy home?

ABOUT US

The CompSoc IEEE NITK Student Branch is a vibrant community of students passionate about Computer Science. We aim to foster a culture of learning and exploration of diverse technologies such as Algorithms, Systems, Machine Learning, Development, Web3, and many more. Through peer discussions and a variety of events, including Knowledge Sharing Sessions, CTFs, and Hackathons, we strive to inspire curiosity and collaboration among our members.
